01 / The Ethos

Not just another player.
A tailored sanctuary for sound.

Wavelet was born out of a desire for simplicity, beauty, and autonomy. A personal music experience crafted for those who value album art as much as lossless frequencies, who want the endless catalog of YouTube Music without commercial clutter, and who cherish local files curated over a lifetime.

01

Fluid Discovery

Browse, search, and stream millions of tracks with YouTube Music innertube integration. Hi-Res FLAC/ALAC audio streaming with smart fallback.

02

Instant Lazy Import

Paste any public or unlisted playlist. Your queue begins playing instantly while background workers lazily import metadata, album art, and tracks.

03

Living Artwork

Dynamic mesh backdrops extract warm, vibrant palettes straight from album canvases. Watch the UI gently shift hues as each track evolves.

02 / The Tactile Charms

The anatomy of Wavelet.

Hover, tilt, and explore the 6 visual totems representing the core engineering principles of the player.

Identity

The Wavelet Mark

Recognizable ruby-red branding, honoring the soul of music notation and natural ripples.

Acoustics

Pure Lossless Audio

Studio-quality bitstreams, custom EQ integration, and granular bit-depth inspection.

Transitions

Automix & Crossfade

Continuous gapless playback with 0–12 second true crossfade curves and beat-matching.

Poetry

Synced Syllables

Apple-style fluid lyric motion with multi-provider fallbacks: LRCLIB, Genius, Musixmatch & KuGou.

Library

Hybrid Catalog

Unify local music folders with cloud streams. Offline downloads with rich embedded ID3 tags.

Flow

Lazy Background Sync

No waiting for 500-track playlists to parse. Start playing on tap while the rest populates quietly.

03 / Capabilities

Crafted with restraint.
Engineered with power.

Every detail in Wavelet is tuned to stay out of your way. No push notifications, no subscription paywalls, no bloated social feeds. Just pure music flowing from your headphones to your mind.

  • ✦
    Apple-Inspired Lyrics Presentation: Fluid, kinetic typography highlighting line-by-line and word-by-word.
  • ✦
    Discord Rich Presence & Scrobbling: Real-time activity broadcasts on Discord, Last.fm, and ListenBrainz.
  • ✦
    Stats For Nerds & Codec Inspector: Direct inspection of bit depth, sample rate, buffer health, and audio engine status.
  • ✦
    Automix DJ Transitions: Smart tempo-stretching and silence trimming for non-stop dance/workout flow.
